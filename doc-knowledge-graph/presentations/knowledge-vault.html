The Problem

Business knowledge lives in wikis, Slack threads, spreadsheets, and tribal memory. When teams need definitions, they get inconsistent answers.

Ambiguous Terms

"CR" means Completion Rate to the Analytics team and Credit Rating to Finance. Without context, queries return the wrong definition.

Hidden Relationships

Retention Rate drives LTV which depends on Revenue Recognition rules. These connections aren't explicit in any single document.

No Source of Truth

Metric calculations vary by team. Is LTV based on billed or recognized revenue? The answer depends on who you ask.

Ontology Design

A seed-driven ontology guides LLM extraction. The top-5 most relevant types are retrieved per note via cosine similarity.

9 Entity Types

  • Dimension
  • Business Rule
  • Persona
  • Data Source
  • Glossary
  • Product
  • Organization
  • System

14 Relationship Types

  • DEPENDS_ON, SLICED_BY
  • GOVERNED_BY, SOURCED_FROM
  • CONSUMED_BY, PRODUCED_BY
  • MEASURES, SERVES, OFFERED_BY
  • SEE_ALSO, SYNONYM_OF
  • REFINES, CONTRADICTS
  • STEERS_QUERY

Why Ontology-Guided?

Without ontology guidance, LLMs hallucinate entity types and relationship names. By embedding the ontology and retrieving the top-5 relevant types per note, we constrain extraction to a consistent, curated vocabulary.

The Knowledge Graph

Two sources of edges produce a rich, interconnected graph.

LLM-Extracted Relationships

The entity extraction model identifies relationships mentioned in the note body. Example: "Retention Rate is the strongest lever for LTV" produces a DEPENDS_ON edge from LTV to Retention Rate.

Explicit see_also Links

Authors declare cross-references in frontmatter. These become SEE_ALSO edges, ensuring important connections survive even if the LLM misses them.

Graph traversal example: Starting from "Activation Rate", a 2-hop traversal discovers Salesforce (data source), Revenue Recognition (business rule), Lead Source (dimension), and Cohort (glossary) — the full context an analyst needs to write a correct query.

Org-Based Disambiguation

The same abbreviation resolves differently based on the user's team context.

Analytics Team

User: "What is CR?"

Agent: "I'm interpreting 'CR' as Completion Rate based on your Analytics team context. If you meant 'Credit Rating' (used by the Finance team), let me know."

Finance Team

User: "What is CR?"

Agent: "I'm interpreting 'CR' as Credit Rating based on your Finance team context. If you meant 'Completion Rate' (used by Analytics), let me know."

How it works: Each concept note has an applicable_orgs array in frontmatter. The user's team is passed in the system prompt. The agent soft-prefers notes whose applicable_orgs matches, and always states its interpretation explicitly.

Next Steps

Add Your Domain

Replace the SaaS concept notes with your organization's metrics, dimensions, and business rules. The pipeline, ontology, and agent are domain-agnostic.

Scale the Vault

The architecture is designed for 50-100+ notes. Add CI/CD with compile_notes.py to auto-deploy on git merge.

Promote to Analyst

Use the Promotion Fragments table to feed verified query pairs and steering rules from the vault into Cortex Analyst semantic views.

Embed in Applications

Call the agent via REST API from Streamlit dashboards, Slack bots, or any application that needs on-demand business context.
