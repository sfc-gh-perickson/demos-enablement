Credit Rating (CR) is an internal scoring metric that assesses the financial risk profile of a channel partner relationship.

## Calculation

Credit Rating is a composite score (1-10) based on:
- Partner's customer growth trajectory (growing/stable/declining)
- Contract renewal probability
- Revenue concentration risk (% of total platform revenue from this partner)
- Payment history and DSO (days sales outstanding)

## Usage

Finance uses Credit Rating to:
- Set revenue reserves for at-risk partnerships
- Prioritize contract renegotiation efforts
- Flag partnerships that may require impairment assessment

## Abbreviation Note

"CR" is used by the Finance team to mean Credit Rating. The Analytics and Customer Success teams use "CR" to mean Completion Rate. When ambiguous, clarify with the requesting team.
