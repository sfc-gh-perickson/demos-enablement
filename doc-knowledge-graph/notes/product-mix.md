Product Mix describes the distribution of customers (or revenue) across different plan types within a reporting period or partner relationship.

## Categories

The primary product mix breakdown:
- **Enterprise** — custom contracts with dedicated support and SLAs
- **Professional** — mid-tier plans with advanced features (monthly or annual billing)
- **Starter** — entry-level plans for individuals and small teams
- **Add-On** — supplemental modules purchased alongside a base plan (e.g., advanced analytics, SSO, premium support)

## Why It Matters

Product Mix is a critical driver of blended metrics. Starter plans have higher activation rates but lower LTV. Enterprise plans have lower activation rates but much higher LTV. Comparing aggregate metrics without controlling for Product Mix produces misleading conclusions.

## Reporting

Always report key metrics (activation rate, retention, revenue) broken out by Product Mix before presenting blended numbers. A shift in mix (e.g., Starter wind-down) can appear as a performance change when it's purely compositional.
