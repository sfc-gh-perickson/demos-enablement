Conversion Rate measures the percentage of all inbound sign-ups that ultimately become paying subscribers, regardless of trial qualification status.

## Calculation

```
Conversion Rate = Paying Subscribers / Total Sign-Ups × 100
```

## Relationship to Activation Rate

Conversion Rate is a broader funnel metric than Activation Rate. Where Activation Rate starts at qualified trials, Conversion Rate includes all sign-ups — even those who never log in after registration.

```
Total Sign-Ups → [Qualification] → Qualified Trials → [Activation] → Subscribers
|___________ Conversion Rate _______________________________________|
                                    |__ Activation Rate _____________|
```

## Segmentation

Always break down by:
- **Lead Source** (organic, paid, referral, partner)
- **Plan** (which plan the sign-up expressed interest in)
- **Channel** (self-serve, sales-assisted, partner-referred)

Low conversion with high activation rate signals a lead quality problem. High conversion with low activation rate signals a product or onboarding problem.
