Contract Start Date is the calendar date on which a customer's subscription term officially begins.

## Why It's Not Obvious

Contract Start Date sounds simple but has several operational meanings:
- **Sign Date**: when the customer signs the contract or accepts terms (may be days/weeks before service begins)
- **Provisioning Date**: when the customer's account is created and access is granted (this is the canonical Contract Start Date)
- **Billing Start Date**: when subscription charges begin (usually aligns with provisioning)

When someone says "start date" without qualification, assume **Provisioning Date**.

## Impact

Contract Start Date drives:
- Revenue recognition timing (recognition begins on provisioning date)
- Cohort assignment (customers are grouped by their start date)
- Retention milestone calculations (Day 1 = first day after start date)
- Sales funnel close date (leads must convert before quarter-end or slip to next period)
