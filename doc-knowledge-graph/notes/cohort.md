A Cohort is a group of customers who begin their subscription during the same time window.

## Why Cohorts Matter

Customer analytics are cohort-based rather than cumulative. This means:
- Customers are grouped by their subscription start period for apples-to-apples comparison
- Performance metrics (completion, retention) are measured at the cohort level
- Growth targets are set per cohort, not per rolling period

## Cohort Naming Convention

Cohorts are identified by: `{Plan Code}-{Start Period YYYY-MM}`

Example: `PRO-2024-01` = Professional plan, January 2024 cohort.

## Cohort vs. Billing Cycle

"Billing Cycle" is the customer's recurring payment schedule. A cohort may span multiple billing cycles or align to one depending on the plan structure. When in doubt, use **cohort** for acquisition analysis and **billing cycle** for revenue reporting.
