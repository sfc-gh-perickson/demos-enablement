Completion Rate (CR) measures the percentage of customers in a cohort who successfully reach full adoption milestones and renew their initial contract term.

## Calculation

```
Completion Rate = Customers Who Completed Term / Customers Who Activated × 100
```

## "Activated" Definition

A customer counts as "activated" once they complete onboarding and perform at least one meaningful action (e.g., running a report or inviting a team member) after the subscription start date. Customers who subscribe but never log in (no-shows) are excluded from the denominator.

## Reporting Lag

Completion Rate is only final after the last customer in a cohort has either renewed or been marked as churned. For monthly plans this is typically 2-4 weeks after the nominal end date (to account for grace periods). For annual contracts, it may take 1-2 additional billing cycles.

## Abbreviation Note

"CR" is used by the Analytics and Customer Success teams to mean Completion Rate. The Finance team uses "CR" to mean Credit Rating. Context determines which is intended.
